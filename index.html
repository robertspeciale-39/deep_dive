How to play

  1. Each dive has 7 questions. You get 25 seconds for each.

  3. Right is not enough. Rarer answers score more. The obvious one is Plankton (10). The deep cut is Leviathan (100).

  5. After each answer you see why and what deeper answers existed. Use it to study.
